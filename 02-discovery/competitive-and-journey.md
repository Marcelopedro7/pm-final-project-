# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Fleet dispatcher coordinating real-time route changes and monitoring drivers across an active fleet.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Maintain a trustworthy real-time view of drivers and deliveries so route changes and reassignments can be made quickly and accurately.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** System and connectivity failures disrupt route management, forcing the dispatcher to rely on WhatsApp, phone calls, and other manual workarounds to keep drivers moving.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** WhatsApp, phone calls, and side spreadsheets or notes.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. Reassign the route in RouteLogic.
2. Send the change through WhatsApp to ensure the driver sees it.
3. Cross-check the dashboard for updated driver status.
4. Call the driver when the information looks outdated or unclear.
5. Manually reconcile any mismatches using notes or spreadsheets.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The dispatcher cannot trust whether the dashboard reflects reality, forcing them to verify route changes and driver status through WhatsApp or phone calls.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** We keep a WhatsApp group as the real system.
