## Set your goal, then your audience

- **Why this goal? what makes this the right goal for this feature right now:** The feature is designed to deepen adoption by making the compliance workflow faster and easier for existing Dispatch Coordinators
- **Target audience the specific segment your goal implies, be precise:** Existing Dispatch Coordinators responsible for completing RouteLogic’s daily compliance workflow.

- **Launch tier S (minimal) · M (targeted) · L (multi-channel) · XL (full GTM):** M — Targeted
- **Justification reach + revenue impact + what silence would risk:** Targeted rollout to existing Dispatch Coordinators across selected customer accounts; requires coordinated product and CS enablement but no broad external campaign or paid media.
- **Is this a launch or a release? does it need go-to-market, or can it just ship?:** Launch

## Define how you'll know it worked
- **Success metrics 2 to 3 metrics that match your GTM goal:** Coordinator compliance-step completion rate (target: ≥60%), One-Click Compliance Checklist adoption rate, and average compliance completion time
- **Bad signal to watch for e.g. high reach, zero signups = message-market mismatch:** Checklist adoption increases but compliance completion remains near the 48% baseline = users are trying the feature, but it is not resolving the core friction; investigate workflow friction and Coordinator feedback before expanding the rollout
- **Most likely post-launch decision double-down · iterate · pivot · deprioritize, and what would trigger it:** Double down and expand the rollout if compliance completion reaches ≥60% while Core Dispatch CSAT remains ≥4.0/5; otherwise iterate based on performance data and Coordinator feedback
