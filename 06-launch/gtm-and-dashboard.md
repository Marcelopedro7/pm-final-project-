# GTM Launch Plan, RouteLogic (B2B)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | One-Click Compliance Checklist |
| Goal | Engagement |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, The feature is designed to deepen adoption by making the compliance workflow faster and easier for existing Dispatch Coordinators.
- **Target audience:** Primary: Dispatch Coordinators using RouteLogic’s daily compliance workflow.

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, Targeted rollout to existing Dispatch Coordinators across selected customer accounts; requires coordinated product and CS enablement but no broad external campaign or paid media

## Channels
1. **Owned: In-app banner**
2. **Owned: Launch email**
3. **Owned: Help Center**

## Enablement & Assets
Quick-start guide, FAQ, demo video, and CS enablement guide.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** PM owns launch coordination, go/no-go decision, post-launch metrics, launch email, and FAQ. Design owns the in-app banner and quick-start guide. Engineering owns production rollout and technical monitoring. CS owns account communication, Coordinator enablement, and feedback collection. PM and Design own the demo video. No additional paid media budget is required.
- **Timeline:** Week 1: finalize launch and enablement assets and brief CS. Week 2: targeted rollout to Dispatch Coordinators via in-app messaging and email. Weeks 3–6: monitor adoption, compliance completion, completion time, CSAT, and user feedback before deciding whether to expand or iterate.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Coordinator compliance-step completion rate (target: ≥60%), One-Click Compliance Checklist adoption rate, and average compliance completion time.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Checklist adoption increases but compliance completion remains near the 48% baseline = users are trying the feature, but it is not resolving the core friction; investigate workflow friction and Coordinator feedback before expanding the rollout.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Double down and expand the rollout if compliance completion reaches ≥60% while Core Dispatch CSAT remains ≥4.0/5; otherwise iterate based on performance data and Coordinator feedback.
