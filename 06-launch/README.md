# RouteLogic Velocity: Simplifying Compliance for Dispatch Coordinators

> A faster, simpler compliance workflow that helps Dispatch Coordinators complete required tasks with less friction and fewer manual workarounds.

## Final Project Deliverables

### Slide 5 · Strategy
- **Problem:** RouteLogic risks customer churn as Dispatch Coordinators struggle with inefficient workflows and rely on manual workarounds to keep daily operations moving
- **Value proposition:** RouteLogic will simplify the compliance workflow with a pre-filled One-Click Compliance Checklist, helping Dispatch Coordinators complete required checks faster and more efficiently
- **Hypothesis:** We believe the One-Click Compliance Checklist will increase Dispatch Coordinator compliance-step completion from 48% to at least 60% (+12pp) within 42 days, while maintaining Core Dispatch CSAT at ≥4.0/5.

### Slide 6 · Research
- **Competitive analysis:** Dispatch Coordinators rely on spreadsheets, mapping tools, WhatsApp, and phone calls when RouteLogic workflows become slow or unreliable. Research revealed that these workarounds create operational friction, while more flexible competing solutions increase RouteLogic’s churn risk.
- **Journey map:** Start shift → Review routes → Handle route changes → Complete compliance tasks ⚠️ (slow, cumbersome, high drop-off) → Coordinate with drivers → Shift handoff

### Slide 8 · Validation
Hypothesis: One-Click Compliance Checklist will increase compliance-step completion by ≥12pp vs. Control.
Control: Current compliance workflow without the checklist.
Variant: Current workflow with the One-Click Compliance Checklist enabled.
Primary metric: Compliance completion rate — baseline 48%, target ≥60%.
Test: 270 users/arm · 50/50 split · 42 days · p < 0.05.
Guardrail: Core Dispatch CSAT ≥4.0/5.
SHIP: ≥12pp improvement vs. Control at p < 0.05 with CSAT ≥4.0/5.
ITERATE: Positive improvement, but <12pp.
KILL: No significant improvement or CSAT falls below 4.0/5.

### Slide 9 · Launch
- **GTM:** Goal: Engagement — deepen adoption by making compliance faster and easier.
Audience: Existing Dispatch Coordinators responsible for RouteLogic’s daily compliance workflow.
Tier: M — Targeted rollout across selected customer accounts.
Channels: Owned — in-app banner, targeted launch email, and Help Center / in-app resource hub.
- **Metrics:** Success metrics: Compliance-step completion rate (target ≥60%), One-Click Compliance Checklist adoption rate, and average compliance completion time.
Guardrail: Core Dispatch CSAT ≥4.0/5.
Bad signal: Checklist adoption increases but compliance completion remains near the 48% baseline — users are trying the feature, but it is not solving the core friction.

### Slide 10 · Story
- **Friction + aha:** Friction point: The hardest part was separating the loudest user pain from the highest-impact product problem without jumping straight to a solution.
Aha moment: The research initially surfaced broad workflow and reliability issues, but the data revealed compliance as the clearest measurable bottleneck. That shifted my focus toward the problem where RouteLogic could create the most measurable impact.
- **Takeaways / next:** Key takeaway: Strong product decisions come from connecting user pain, business impact, and measurable evidence — and being willing to change direction when the data reveals a higher-impact problem.
What I’d do next: Run the 42-day A/B experiment, combine the quantitative results with Dispatch Coordinator feedback, and expand the rollout only if compliance completion reaches the predefined success criteria without harming Core Dispatch CSAT.
